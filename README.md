## Project Overview

The **Apple Product Price Trends & Market Insights Dashboard (2020–2026)** is an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard developed to analyze Apple product pricing, customer reviews, discounts, ratings, sales events, and stock availability across multiple e-commerce platforms.

The project transforms raw pricing data into actionable business insights through data cleaning, Pivot Tables, KPI reporting, and interactive visualizations, enabling users to monitor pricing trends and evaluate product performance over time.

---

## Business Problem

Retail pricing and customer engagement data often exist in large, unstructured datasets that are difficult to interpret. This project addresses that challenge by providing an interactive dashboard that enables stakeholders to:

- Monitor product pricing trends.
- Evaluate customer ratings and reviews.
- Analyze discount performance.
- Compare product categories.
- Track stock availability across platforms.
- Support pricing and marketing decisions.

---

## Project Objectives

- Analyze Apple product pricing trends from 2020 to 2026.
- Evaluate customer ratings across product categories.
- Compare discount performance among Apple products.
- Identify the most reviewed Apple products.
- Monitor stock availability across platforms.
- Examine pricing behavior across months and weekdays.
- Develop an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

---

## Key Insights

- iPhone products consistently received the highest customer ratings.
- Pricing trends remained relatively stable throughout most months, with noticeable fluctuations during selected periods.
- Promotional events contributed significantly to higher discount percentages across product categories.
- Certain Apple models generated substantially higher customer engagement through review counts.
- Stock availability differed across platforms, highlighting differences in inventory distribution.
